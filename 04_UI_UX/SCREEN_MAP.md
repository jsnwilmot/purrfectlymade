## Screen purpose

- The website should let visitors browse handmade craft items in a clean and simple layout. Each product or product category should include a clear image, item name, short description, and optional notes such as custom colours, personalization, or availability.
- The product management approach must be simple enough for the young client and family support to update. Products should be managed through a clear structure, such as an easy product data file, simple content folder, or lightweight admin-friendly process. The site should not require complex coding for every small product update.
- The request form should let a customer submit their name, email, item they are interested in, custom request details, and optional message. The form should send the request to an approved parent or business email.
- The Facebook contact option should be visible but should not display a personal phone number.

## Primary users

- The target users are customers who want to view and request handmade craft items, including jewelry, bracelets, keychains, resin pieces, 3D printed items, seasonal gifts, and personalized items. The site will also be used by the business owner and family support members who help manage products, requests, and customer communication.

## Key components

- The initial website must include:
- Homepage with brand introduction and featured product highlights.
- Product showcase where visitors can view handmade items such as bracelets, jewelry, keychains, resin pieces, 3D printed items, seasonal gifts, and custom crafts.
- Simple product management structure so items can be added, edited, hidden, or removed easily.
- Request form for customers to ask about buying an item or placing a custom order.
- Facebook messaging link as an approved contact option.
- About section that explains Purrfectly Made in a safe, polished, family-supported way.
- Gallery or product photo sections for approved product images.
- Basic SEO setup for handmade crafts, gifts, jewelry, and local handmade items.
- Mobile-friendly responsive layout.
- Favicon and Open Graph social sharing image.
- Use of the approved logo and provided graphic assets.

## Inputs

- Product photos.
- Product names.
- Product categories.
- Product descriptions.
- Availability status.

## Outputs

- Customer understands what items are available and how to request one.

## Navigation notes

- The website must support these main workflows:
- Customer browses products
- Customer submits a purchase or custom order request
- Parent or administrator reviews a request
- Product item is added to the website
- Product item is updated
- Product item is hidden or removed
- Customer uses Facebook messaging
- Seasonal or featured product update
- Form error or failed submission handling

## Access notes

- Customer:
- Can view public pages and submit request forms. Customers cannot edit products, view other requests, access private data, or create accounts.
- Business Owner:
- Can provide product details, approve product listings, and help decide what appears on the site. Any access to private customer messages should be supervised by a parent or guardian.
- Parent or Guardian:
- Can review customer requests, manage customer communication, approve public content, and control business contact details.
- Site Administrator:
- Can update website files, products, images, forms, SEO metadata, favicon, Open Graph image, and deployment settings.

## Accessibility notes

- The website should be easy to use on mobile, tablet, and desktop. Text should be readable, buttons and links should be clear, and navigation should be simple. Product images should include descriptive alt text where possible. Form fields should have clear labels, required fields should be easy to understand, and the site should support keyboard navigation and screen reader basics.
