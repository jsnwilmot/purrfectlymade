Care Tips

Simple care for handmade pieces

Handmade items last longer with gentle use, dry storage, and a little extra care.

Beaded bracelets

Roll bracelets on gently instead of pulling hard. Keep them dry and store them flat or in a small pouch.

Keychains

Avoid heavy tugging on charms or tassels. Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth if needed.

Resin pieces

Keep resin away from high heat and long direct sun. Clean with a soft cloth and mild care.

Earrings

Store earrings in a dry place. Clean posts or hooks with appropriate jewelry care before wearing.

3D printed items

Keep 3D printed pieces away from heat, pressure, and rough play. Wipe lightly when dusty.

Handmade gifts

Small handmade pieces may include tiny parts. Keep them away from very young children unless supervised.
