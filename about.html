About Purrfectly Made

Purrfectly Made is a local kid-owned handmade craft business in the Lethbridge, Alberta area. Each piece is made with care, creativity, and a love for small thoughtful gifts.

Handmade and personal

Purrfectly Made creates bracelets, keychains, earrings, resin pieces, 3D printed items, seasonal gifts, and personalized craft-style items. Colours, sizes, and availability may vary because each piece is handmade.

Request-first, family-safe

  • All requests are reviewed by a parent or guardian before any order is confirmed.
  • The site is a showcase, not a checkout store.
  • There is no cart, payment collection, or direct contact listing.
  • The only contact method on this website is the request form.
