Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before requesting

Clear family-friendly details about custom requests, availability, review, and handmade care.

Can I buy directly from the website?

No. This website is a showcase and request form only. There is no cart, checkout, payment collection, or customer account.

How do I request an item?

Use the Request an Item form and share the item type, preferred colours, and a short message. A parent or guardian reviews every request before anything is confirmed.

Are custom colours available?

Custom colours can be requested when supplies and timing allow. Handmade colours may vary slightly from example images.

Are items always in stock?

No. Availability may change because each item is handmade.

Who reviews requests?

All requests are reviewed by a parent or guardian before any order is confirmed.

Where is Purrfectly Made located?

Purrfectly Made is a local kid-owned handmade craft business in the Lethbridge, Alberta area.

Do you ship?

Shipping or delivery options can be discussed after a request is reviewed by a parent or guardian.

Are items handmade?

Yes. Items are handmade, so colours, sizing, and small details may vary.

Are small pieces safe for young children?

Some handmade gifts may include small parts and are not intended for very young children. Adult supervision is recommended.
